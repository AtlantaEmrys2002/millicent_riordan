Current Projects

Developing an Autonomous Retrieval Robot

My team have constructed a Leo Rover and are in the process of implementing custom object detection, navigation, and manipulator software that allows the robot to autonomously traverse unknown environments, retrieve objects, and return them to various zones.

Previous Projects

Simulating and Discovering New High-Energy Astronomical Objects

Over a six-month research period, I developed new methods for simulating Fermi Large Area Telescope (LAT) observations of the γ -ray sky. After creating realistic simulations of active galactic nuclei and pulsars, I applied 22 novel machine and deep learning-based source extraction algorithms to real Fermi observations. 11 of my novel algorithms independently discovered >98% of the sources already included in the 4FGL catalog. As the first author to apply ML and DL source extraction algorithms to real Fermi data, I discovered thousands of new γ-ray source candidates.

Designing ISL Topologies for LEO Satellite Networks

I created novel algorithms for constructing low-latency, resilient, and stable inter-satellite link (ISL) network topologies for satellite mega-constellations in low Earth orbit (LEO). These novel algorithms improved on the state-of-the art with latency reduced by 47% for constellations such as Starlink and mean hop count reduced by 52% - 73%.

Training a Robot to Navigate Extreme Terrain

I utilised off-policy, model-free reinforcement learning techniques to train a simulated bipedal robot to navigate difficult terrain as quickly as possible. The final algorithm was a heavily modified version of the Twin Delayed Deep Deterministic (TD3) algorithm. The robot learnt to run, jump, and hop in the environment in fewer than 1500 training epochs.

Breaking Discrete Logarithm-Based Codes

For my third year cryptography coursework, I built efficient, fast implementations of factorisation algorithms to hack discrete logarithm-based cryptographic schemes, such as the Diffie-Hellman Key Exchange and ElGamal cryptosystem.
