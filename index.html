About Me

I am a first year Probationer Research Student (DPhil candidate), studying at the University of Oxford. Associated with the EPSRC Centre for Doctoral Training in Robotics and AI for Net Zero, my research will focus on agile motion planning with energy-storage actuators. Currently, I am based at the University of Manchester, completing the taught component of a MSc Robotics programme.

Research Interests

As shown by my academic background (which covers the fields of computer science, astrophysics, and engineering), I prefer multi-disciplinary research and pooling knowledge to make the world a better place.

My technological interests include:

  • Computer Vision
  • High Performance Computing
  • Networking
  • Reinforcement Learning
Many of my previous projects involve the application of these concepts to space-based problems. However, my present ambition is to apply them to decarbonisation and sustainability problems which involve robotics.
